Privacy Policy — LPU Auto Login

Last updated: August 14, 2026
🔒 This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything stays on your device.

What the extension does

This extension automatically fills in and submits the login form on internet.lpu.in (LPU's internet/Wi-Fi authentication portal) using a username and password that you provide.

What data is stored, and where

  • The only data handled is the username and password you type into the popup.
  • It is saved using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API.
  • It is stored locally on your device only, inside your browser profile.
  • It is never sent to a remote server, database, analytics service, or third party.
  • It is never synced to the cloud (the extension does not use chrome.storage.sync).

What the extension does NOT do

  • No network requests, APIs, or servers of its own.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting.
  • No reading or logging of browsing history or activity on other sites.
  • No sharing of data with the developer or anyone else.

Permissions explained

  • storage — saves your username/password locally so you don't re-enter them each time.
  • activeTab / scripting — fills in and submits the login form on the LPU portal page.
  • host_permissions (internet.lpu.in) — the content script only runs on this domain, nowhere else.

Removing your data

Click "Clear saved credentials" in the extension popup at any time, or uninstall the extension to remove all locally stored data.
